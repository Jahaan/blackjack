How it's computed. An exact recursive solver evaluates the dealer's draw tree under hit-soft-17, then finds win / push / lose probability and EV for standing, hitting, doubling, splitting, and surrendering. Deck composition is a 6-deck shoe with your three known cards removed. Splitting assumes one re-split is not taken and each new hand may double (DAS); other actions are exact. Odds describe this single hand against a fresh dealer up card — not a betting system. Gamble responsibly.
