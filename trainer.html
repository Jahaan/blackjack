Hand history actual vs theoretical
Theo · perfect = your bet × the value of the 3 dealt cards (your two + dealer's upcard) played perfectly, including the chance the dealer has blackjack. Theo · your play = the same minus what each misplay cost. Actual − your play is the luck of the draw. Bet sizing = (your bet − ramp bet) × your edge at that true count (≈ −0.55% + 0.5% per TC); negative means it cost you. Values come from a built-in blackjack calculator for these rules; it doesn't adjust playing values for the count.

Counting
True count = running count ÷ decks remaining. Running count answers must be exact; true count answers are accepted if rounded, truncated, or within ±0.5. Deviations use the Illustrious 18 & Fab 4 indices.
Bet ramp: bet (true count − 1) units, minimum 1, up to your spread. TC +2 → 1, +3 → 2, +4 → 3, +5 → 4… Graded on the true count floored or rounded; within 1 unit counts as right (within 2 from TC +5 up).
